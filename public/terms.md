# House Rules
## The Repair Cafe is **FREE**
* Work carried out in the Sutton Repair Café (SRC) is performed free of charge on a voluntary basis by repair experts.
* New materials such as leads, plugs, fuses, patches or other parts used on repairs may be charged separately (at cost).
* A voluntary donation is greatly appreciated.

## Liability
* Sutton Repair Café volunteers are NOT liable for any loss resulting from advice/instructions/work concerning repairs or for the loss of items handed over for repair.*
* Visitors offer broken items for repair at their own risk. 
* Repair experts offer no guarantee for the repairs carried out and are NOT liable if objects that are repaired subsequently fail to work properly.

## Repairing Items
* Repair experts are entitled to refuse to repair certain objects. 
* Repair experts are not obliged to reassemble disassembled appliances that cannot be repaired. 
* To cut down on waiting times during busy periods, ONE broken item per person will be examined; the visitor will join the back of the queue if there is a second item for repair.
* If an item cannot be fully repaired at a Repair Café session due to lack of time (for example) the repairer may offer to complete the repair at home. In such cases the repairer and customer must exchange contact details and neither SRC nor the repairer assume any responsibility for safe-keeping.

## Items which cannot be repaired
* Visitors to the Sutton Repair Café are responsible for the tidy removal of broken or disassembled objects that could not be repaired.

## Photos and video
* We sometimes take photos and video at our events for use in our social media and other publicity. 
* By coming to our Repair Cafe, you are consenting to any pictures or videos taken, being used in our social media and other publicity used for marketing.
