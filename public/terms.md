# Terms & Conditions and House Rules

> **Welcome to the Repair Café!**
> Our twofold mission is **diverting waste from landfills** and **reviving practical repair skills** in our local community. We are a volunteer-run, non-profit community initiative. Please read these terms and house rules carefully before registering your item.

---

## 1. Volunteer & Best-Effort Basis
* **Community Best-Effort Basis:** All diagnostic inspections, practical repairs, and advice are provided solely by unpaid volunteer repairers ("fixers") acting in good faith on a best-effort basis.
* **No Commercial Warranty:** The Repair Café does not offer any commercial guarantee, warranty of merchantability, or promise of successful repair.
* **Co-Repair & Learning Ethos:** The Repair Café is not a free drop-off repair shop. Visitors are encouraged to sit alongside the volunteer fixer during diagnosis and repair to observe, ask questions, and learn practical repair skills.

---

## 2. Electrical Safety & Mandatory PAT Testing
* **Dual-Phase Electrical Safety Screening:** All 240V AC mains-powered electrical appliances must undergo safety inspection under the IET Code of Practice for In-service Inspection and Testing of Electrical Equipment (Portable Appliance Testing / PAT):
  1. **Pre-Repair Inspection (PAT 1):** Qualified safety testers perform visual checks, BS 1363 plug fuse rating checks, insulation resistance tests, and Class I earth continuity measurements before fixers work on your device.
  2. **Post-Repair Certification (PAT 2):** Any repaired mains appliance must undergo mandatory post-repair electrical safety verification before being signed off and returned to the visitor.
* **Prohibited Items & Immediate Quarantine:** Fixers and safety testers reserve the absolute right to refuse to inspect or repair any device deemed intrinsically hazardous (including leaking batteries, devices with suspected asbestos, microwave ovens with damaged door seals, or appliances presenting severe shock or fire hazards). Items tagged `DANGER_DO_NOT_ENERGIZE` are quarantined immediately and will not be plugged into mains power under any circumstances.
* **Damaged Power Cords & Plugs:** Mains items failing initial PAT testing due to cord or plug defects (`FAIL_REPAIRABLE_CORD`) are flagged with an electrical safety caution banner and must only be repaired whilst de-energized.

---

## 3. Limitation of Liability & Assumption of Risk
* **Customer Assumption of Risk:** Visitors submit their property to the Repair Café entirely at their own risk.
* **Disclaimer of Liability:** To the maximum extent permitted by UK law, neither the Repair Café organizing committee, the venue operators, nor individual volunteer fixers accept liability for:
  * Any direct, indirect, or consequential loss or damage arising during transit, handling, diagnostic testing, or repair.
  * Latent, progressive, or pre-existing defects worsened during disassembly, diagnosis, or testing.
  * Loss of digital data, memory cards, or stored media. Visitors are strictly advised to back up all digital data before bringing electronic equipment.

---

## 4. Replacement Parts & Consumables
* **Visitor-Provided Parts:** Visitors are responsible for sourcing and purchasing necessary replacement components (e.g. zips, custom drive belts, specific thermal fuses, power cords, replacement batteries, or screen assemblies).
* **Consumables Contribution:** Fixers may supply standard consumables (such as solder, heat-shrink tubing, basic hookup wire, lubricating oil, or sewing thread) without charge, though voluntary donations towards consumable replenishment are appreciated.

---

## 5. Fair Queueing & Single-Item Registration
* **Fair Queue Policy:** To ensure fair access for all members of the community, registration is limited to **one item per person at a time** (unless explicitly indicated otherwise by front desk organizers).
* **Multiple Items:** Visitors with more than one broken item must check in their first item, wait for its completion or sign-out, and re-join the registration queue for subsequent items.

---

## 6. Custody, Collection & "Taken Home" Arrangements
* **Same-Day Collection:** All items must be collected from the Front Desk before the scheduled close of the session. The Repair Café has no venue storage facilities and cannot hold items between monthly sessions. Uncollected items may be recycled or disposed of if not retrieved by the end of the day.
* **Informal Home Repairs (`TAKEN_HOME`):** Where a volunteer fixer generously offers to take an item home to finish a complex repair outside session hours, this is a private, informal arrangement between the visitor and the volunteer. The Repair Café ceases to hold organizational custody, responsibility, or insurance coverage for the item once it is signed out from the Front Desk.

---

## 7. Workshop Safety & Supervision of Minors
* **Accompanied Children:** Children are warmly encouraged to attend and learn, but must remain supervised by a responsible adult at all times.
* **Active Workshop Environment:** Fixer stations use high-temperature soldering irons, rotary cutting tools, sharp blades, and chemical solvents. Visitors must exercise care, stay behind safety markers, and follow all safety instructions given by organizers and fixers.

---

## 8. Data Protection & UK GDPR Compliance
* **Limited On-Day Use:** Personal details (first name, optional telephone number) are collected strictly for on-day queue management, card token matching, and SMS/call collection notifications.
* **Automated Redaction:** In compliance with UK GDPR Article 5(1)(e), customer identifying contact details are automatically redacted to `[REDACTED]` when the session is finalized by organizers.
* **Anonymous Impact Reporting:** Statistical data (item category, weight diverted from landfill, fault diagnosis, and repair duration) is retained anonymously for council and environmental grant impact reports.
